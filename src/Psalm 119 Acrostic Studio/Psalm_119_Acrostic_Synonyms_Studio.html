The Context of Affliction

Psalm 119 Was Composed in the Shadows

Many assume Psalm 119 is dry legalism written in an ivory tower. In truth, over 65 verses describe the psalmist being mocked, surrounded by corrupt rulers, falsely accused, facing prison pits, and near death. Torah devotion was their sanity lifeline amid intense suffering!

1. Like a Wineskin in the Smoke
"For I have become like a wineskin in the smoke, yet I have not forgotten your statutes." (v. 83)

Shriveled, blackened, and dried out by suffering, yet clinging to God's instruction.

2. Pits Dug by the Insolent
"The insolent have dug pitfalls for me; they do not conform to your law... They have almost made an end of me on earth." (vv. 85, 87)

Facing legal ambush and physical entrapment from corrupt totalitarian authorities.

3. Smeared with Lies
"The insolent smear me with lies, but with my whole heart I keep your precepts; their heart is unfeeling like fat." (vv. 69–70)

Enduring weaponized disinformation while anchoring the heart in divine truth.
