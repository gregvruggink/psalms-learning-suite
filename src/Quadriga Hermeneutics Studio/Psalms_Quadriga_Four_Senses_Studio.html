Dr. Patrick Schreiner Masterclass Reference

"When David prayed, whose voice do we hear? David's, Christ's, or the Church's?"

The Christian church historically resolved this through the Four Senses of Scripture (*Quadriga*). Far from medieval fantasy, when anchored in Reformation grammatical-historical guardrails, the four senses show how Jesus Christ is the eternal archetype of David's prayers, preserving the ancient history while unlocking the gospel.

4. Anagogical Sense (The Roof & Horizon) Speranda (What to Hope For)

Eschatological Consummation & New Creation

Directs the heart toward Christ's final return, cosmic judgment, and the restoration of all things.

3. Tropological Sense (The Interior Furnishings) Agenda (What to Do)

Christian Ethics & Soul Formation in Union with Christ

How believers live today—valid ONLY when derived through union with Christ (Head to Body).

2. Christological / Allegorical Sense (The Walls) Credenda (What to Believe)

Christ the Archetype: Cross, Resurrection & Church

How David’s office, cries, and victories witness to Jesus Christ and His bride (Totus Christus).

1. Literal / Historical Sense (The Foundation) Historia (What Happened)

Hebrew Grammar, Covenant History & Ancient Setting

The indispensable anchor: grammar, syntax, historical battles, and ancient Davidic kingship.

The Sacred Sequencing of Grace
Literal (Historia) ➔ Christological (Credenda) ➔ Tropological (Agenda)

Why Sequence Matters: You can only apply the psalm’s moral demands to the believer after filtering through Christ. Christ kept the psalm first. We inhabit its righteousness only through union with Him.

The Moralism Trap (Skipping Christ)
Literal (David) ──────[SKIPS CHRIST]─────► Moralism ("Dare to be a David")

If you jump from David directly to modern application without Christ, you produce exhausting works-righteousness and guilt. Jesus is the true Psalm-singer; we sing in Him.
